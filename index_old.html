Privacy Policy

Chess – Play Against Computer · Varala Studios
Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Chess – Play Against Computer (the "App"), published by Varala Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: we do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The App does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences (difficulty level, piece collection, board theme, sound and volume settings, move highlighting and animation options) in your device's local storage so they are remembered the next time you play. This information stays on your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Internet access and third parties

The App works entirely offline. The computer opponent runs on your device, and the App does not connect to any server. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking tools, no social media features and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Because nothing is transmitted, no information is shared with or sold to third parties.

4. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or any other sensitive permission. Game sounds are generated on your device and played through your normal audio output.

5. Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The App is suitable for all ages and contains no in-app purchases, ads or external links.

6. Data security and retention

Since we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to secure, retain or delete on our side. Your saved preferences remain on your own device under your control.

7. Your rights

You can delete all information the App stores at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. As we hold no personal data about you, there is no data for us to access, correct or erase on request.

8. Store platforms

If you download the App through Google Play or the Apple App Store, those platforms may collect information about your download and device in line with their own privacy policies. We do not receive or control that information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above. If a future version of the App were to collect any information, this policy would be updated before that change is released.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Varala Studios at varalastudios@gmail.com.
