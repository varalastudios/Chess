Privacy Policy

Chess – Play Against Computer · Varala Studios
Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Chess – Play Against Computer (the "App"), published by Varala Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: we do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any account details, and the App has no accounts, no chat, no ads and no analytics. The only time the App uses the internet is the optional "Play with a Friend" mode, described below.

1. Playing against the computer

When you play against the computer, everything happens on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the App works fully offline.

2. Playing with a friend online (optional)

"Play with a Friend" lets two people play on different devices using a short game code. There is no sign-in and no chat. To make this work, the App sends the following to a game server run on Google Firebase Realtime Database:

  • a randomly generated game code and a random player identifier that is created for each game and is not linked to you, your device or your accounts;
  • the chess moves made in the game;
  • which side each player takes, and simple status signals (for example that a player resigned, left, asked for a rematch, or is still connected);
  • a reaction, when you tap one of four emoji buttons (happy, sad, thumbs up, thumbs down).

Players cannot type or send text, photos, voice or any other content to each other. This information is used only to show the moves and reactions to the other player. Like any internet service, the server provider receives your device's IP address as part of the connection; we do not use it and we do not have access to it. Game information is kept only as long as needed to run the game, is not linked to any identity, and may be deleted at any time. You can turn off the display of friends' reactions in Settings.

Anyone who knows a game code can join that game while it has a free place, so only share your code with the person you want to play with.

3. Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences (difficulty level, piece collection, board theme, sound and volume settings, move highlighting, animation and reaction options) in your device's local storage so they are remembered the next time you play. This information stays on your device and is removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

4. What we do not do

We do not show advertising, use analytics or tracking tools, create user profiles, sell or share personal information, or ask for any account. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game. It does not ask for any personal information, has no chat or free-text messaging, no ads and no in-app purchases. Because players can only exchange chess moves and four fixed emoji, children cannot send or receive messages from strangers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians who have questions can contact us at the address below.

6. Data security and retention

Connections to the game server are encrypted. We do not hold any personal information about you. Your saved preferences remain on your own device under your control.

7. Your rights

You can delete everything the App stores on your device at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or erase on request. If you believe a game you took part in should be deleted, contact us with the game code and we will remove it.

8. Store platforms and service providers

If you download the App through Google Play or the Apple App Store, those platforms may collect information about your download and device under their own privacy policies. The online game server is provided by Google (Firebase); its handling of connection data is described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive or control that information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above. We will update this policy before releasing any change to the App that collects additional information.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Varala Studios at varalastudios@gmail.com.
